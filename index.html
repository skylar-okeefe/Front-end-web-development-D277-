About Washington

Washington, known as The Evergreen State, is located in the Pacific Northwest region of the United States. It is renowned for its diverse landscapes, ranging from lush forests and rugged coastlines to majestic mountains and arid deserts. The state is home to vibrant cities like Seattle, known for its tech industry and cultural landmarks, as well as beautiful natural attractions such as Mount Rainier and the Puget Sound.
