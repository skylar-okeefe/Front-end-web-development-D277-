About Spokane


Spokane, known as the "Lilac City" is the largest city in Eastern Washington. Located in the Inland Northwest, it is known for its outdoor recreational opportunities, iconic landmarks like the Spokane Falls, and its role as a hub for healthcare and education.


  • Region: Eastern Washington, Inland Northwest
  • Classification:Spokane is classified as Urban. It is the 2nd most populous city in Washington.
  • Population: Approximately 230,000 residents
  • Incorporation: The city was officially incorporated on November 29, 1881
  • Income Level: The average income in Spokane is around $73,500, which is significantly lower than the state average of $95,160.

Top 3 Attractions

  1. Riverfront park & Spokane Falls: 100 acre park with views of the largest urban waterfall in the United States
  2. Spokane Hoopfest: Worlds largest 3 on 3 basketball tournament
  3. Manito Park: A beautiful park with gardens.

Major Industries

  • Healthcare and Medical Research
  • Education
  • Manufacturing and Technology

Did You Know?

Spokane hosted the first environmentally themed World's Fair, Expo '74, which focused on the environment and conservation. Spokane is also known as the birthplace of Father's Day, established in 1910 by Sonora Smart Dodd.
