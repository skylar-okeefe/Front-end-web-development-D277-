About Olympia

Olympia, nicknamed "Oly"is the capital of Washington State. Located in the Pacific Northwest, it is famous for its vibrant arts scene, iconic landmarks like the State Capitol, and its role as a hub for government and education.

  • Region: Western Washington, Puget Sound
  • Classification:Olympia is classified as Urban. It is the 8th most populous city in Washington.
  • Population: Approximately 56,689 residents
  • Incorporation: The city was officially incorporated on January 28, 1859
  • Income Level: The average income in Olympia is significantly lower than the state average

Top 3 landmarks

  2. Pike Place Market
  3. Chihuly Garden and Glass
