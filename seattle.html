About Seattle

Seattle, known as the "Emerald City," is the largest city in Washington State. Located in the Pacific Northwest, it is famous for its vibrant arts scene, iconic landmarks like the Space Needle, and its role as a hub for technology companies such as Amazon and Microsoft.

  • Region: Western Washington, Puget Sound
  • Classification:Seattle is classified as Urban. It is the largest city in Washington.
  • Population: Approximately 816,600 residents
  • Incorporation: The city was officially incorporated on December 2, 1869
  • Income Level: The average income in Seattle is significantly higher than the state average

Top 3 landmarks

  1. Space Needle
  2. Pike Place Market
  3. Chihuly Garden and Glass

Major Industries

  • Technology - Cloud Computing and Software Development
  • Healthcare
  • Aerospace and Aviation

Did You Know?

Seattle is home to the first Starbucks coffee shop, which opened in 1971 at Pike Place Market.
